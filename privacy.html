Privacy policy

Updated October 24, 2026.

anjbud is a private Google account integration for tasks authorized by the connected account owner. It is not a public signup service.

Data used for requested tasks

With the account owner's permission, installed tools can access the Google data needed for a task. Depending on the granted permissions, this can include Gmail messages and attachments; calendar events; Drive files and metadata; Docs, Sheets, and Slides content; contacts; and YouTube channel, video, and upload data.

The integration uses that data to complete the owner's requested work, such as finding information, preparing or editing files, managing events, or handling authorized communications and uploads. The permissions granted to an account determine the available operations.

Storage and processing

OAuth tokens are stored in the local operating-system credential stores used by the installed tools. Google account data may be retained in private task records, files, and workspaces where it is needed for the owner's work.

Data may also be processed by tools or services selected by the account owner to complete the requested task. Processing can take place outside the local device. The owner controls which tools and services are used and which data is provided to them.

Limits on use

anjbud does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

anjbud's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and account control

The account owner controls retained task records in the private files and workspaces where the work is performed. Retention depends on the task and the owner's decisions; the integration does not apply one automatic deletion period to every task.

The owner can review or revoke the integration's access in Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future access under that grant. Copies already retained in private task records can be removed separately by the owner.

This website

These static pages do not collect Google account data or OAuth tokens. They contain no analytics, forms, client-side scripts, or cookies.
