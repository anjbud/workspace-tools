anjbud

anjbud is a private integration that lets installed tools carry out tasks authorized by the owner of a connected Google account. It has no public signup.

Depending on the permissions granted, those tasks can include working with Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Contacts, and YouTube. The account owner chooses the work and the tools or services used to complete it.

OAuth tokens are kept in the local operating-system credential stores used by the installed tools. Requested work may use Google account data in private task records and owner-selected tools or services.

Read the privacy policy for data handling details and the terms for the scope of this private integration.
