anjbud is a private integration for tasks authorized by the owner of a connected Google account. This website describes the integration; it does not offer accounts, subscriptions, or a public service.

Authorized use

The integration connects installed tools to Google services using permissions granted by the account owner. Available services can include Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Contacts, and YouTube. Work is directed by the account owner and must stay within that owner's authorization.

Permissions and control

The account owner can review or revoke access through Google Account connections. Granting access enables the permitted operations; instructions from the owner determine which tasks the installed tools should perform.

The integration uses Google's existing services and the tools or services selected by the owner. Their applicable terms continue to govern their use.

Data handling

The privacy policy explains how Google account data and credentials are used, retained, and controlled for this private workflow.
